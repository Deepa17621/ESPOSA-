Key Architectural Decisions

CRM Architecture

1

Single Zoho CRM Org

All countries, stores, brands in one organisation. Country/store segregated by config, roles, and permissions.

2

Base Currency: USD

Per-opportunity local currency (AED, SAR, LBP). Admin-only exchange rates. Cannot change once set.

3

ERP-Independent Design

Minimal Noria customisation. CRM built so Noria can be replaced post-project without restructuring.

4

Booking Engine: Zoho Creator App

Separate custom app, connected to CRM. Opened via button on CRM Opportunity record.

Business Rules

1

CRM Never Invoices

All invoicing in Noria ERP. CRM sends Sales Orders and receives payment status back via webhook.

2

Wholesale Out of Scope

Only retail in CRM. Vendor/wholesale flows remain entirely in Noria.

3

Multi-Fitting Post-Sale

Journey continues long after Sales Order: fittings, alterations, trials. Final invoice only on delivery day.

4

Human-Led Confirmation

All appointment confirmations are manual human steps (call + WhatsApp). No fully automated confirmation.

Try & Take

Usually final fitting. Bride tries and takes dress. May repeat if minor work still needed.

Only Take / Dresser / Delivery

Collection only — no fitting room or consultant needed. Handled as a task/reminder. Driver may collect.
